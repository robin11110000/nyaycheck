## Summary spot checks

- [Emirates v Srikanta Saha, FA 94/2013](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/188547247/): final paragraph preserves the ₹4,24,280 airfare refund and sets compensation at ₹30,000. The seed excludes interest and leaves unresolved costs null.
- [Amazon v Jaspreet Kaur, FA 1004/2022](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/188026850/): the provider appeal is dismissed and the consumer relief is upheld. The seed correctly records substantive consumer relief, rather than treating the dismissed appeal as a failed consumer claim; legal-aid damages are excluded.
- [Aashish Oberai v Emaar, CC 70/2015](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/173629696/): final direction orders ₹1,15,78,537 principal refund and ₹10,000 litigation costs. Formula-dependent interest stays excluded.

HTTP success proves source accessibility, not the correctness of every interpretation. These three checks confirm the summaries and cited operative amounts; the rest of the corpus retains its earlier source review.
